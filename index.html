Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does junk removal cost in Xenia and Dayton?

Price depends on how much space your items take up in our 16 cubic yard trailer. It starts at $99 for about 1 cubic yard and goes up to $600 for a full trailer. Hot tubs, sheds, decks, concrete, tires, and refrigerators are priced separately. You approve the price before we start.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Often, yes, when our schedule allows. Message us early in the day and we will confirm.

Do I have to clean up or sort anything first?

No. Leave it where it is. We do the lifting, loading, and hauling. You just point.

Which items cost extra?

Concrete, dirt, brick and asphalt, shingles and construction debris, heavy items such as hot tubs, pianos and safes, tires, refrigerators and freezers, and large sheds, decks and above-ground pools are priced separately. For hazardous materials, ask us first.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Xenia, Dayton, Washington Court House, Beavercreek, Bellbrook, Centerville, Fairborn, and everywhere within about 30 miles.

Can you handle an estate or hoarding cleanout?

Yes. We work patiently and without judgment, sort and donate what we can, and haul the rest on your timeline.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Your property is protected on every job.
